What the platform does from day one — and what Phase 2 adds

This page sums up the scope from your seat: every line says what you, your team or your drivers will be able to do. The journey of a load

The 7 stages you live every day, from posting the truck to closing the load.

1
🚚

I post my truck

From day one
  • ✓I post each truck's availability: date, location, preferred lanes, equipment type and minimum rate per mile.
  • ✓I declare the real capacity: pallets and weight, FTL or LTL (whether it takes partial loads), reefer temperature range and securement (chains, straps, tarps).
  • ✓My brokers receive the availability by email, automatically — and their replies flow straight into the system.
Phase 2
  • Brokers log into the platform with their own accounts and post their loads directly (self-service portal).
2
🔎

I land the load

⚖ includes a decision that's yours to make
From day one
  • ✓DAT loads come into the system and get ranked against my available truck. How they come in — automatically via API, AI-assisted (you copy/paste or upload a screenshot) or quick manual entry — depends on the option you choose. That decision is yours and is still pending.
  • ✓The broker's rate confirmation (PDF, any format) is read by AI; I just review, fix if needed and sign electronically on screen. Goodbye print, hand-sign and scan.
  • ✓Multi-stop loads supported (several pickups and several deliveries on the same load).
Phase 2
  • Negotiation with the broker inside the platform (offers and counter-offers with full history).
  • More load boards beyond DAT (Truckstop, 123Loadboard).
  • Broker credit data at a glance (Ansonia / TransCredit).
3
📦

I dispatch

From day one
  • ✓The signed rate confirmation becomes an order; I assign driver + truck + trailer and the system validates equipment compatibility, capacity and compliance before letting me dispatch.
  • ✓Multi-load: one trip can carry several loads (LTL) and I decide the stop sequence.
  • ✓The driver gets everything in his app and confirms he saw it (acknowledgment). He never sees rates or financial information.
Phase 2
This stage ships complete in v1.
4
🛰️

On the road

From day one
  • ✓I see the truck on the map from the moment the driver accepts — not from pickup: ETA against the appointment, and alerts if he's running late or hasn't moved for a while.
  • ✓Automatic check-in on arrival at the shipper (geofence); BOL, seal number and weight tickets captured with the camera.
  • ✓Detention >2h detected and documented on its own — with time, GPS and photos — at pickup and at delivery. Evidence ready to bill it.
  • ✓The driver reports statuses (fuel stop, rest break, traffic delay) and the reefer temperature from his app.
Phase 2
  • Sensors and telematics: automatic reefer temperature, ELD / Hours of Service, automatic route-deviation detection.
5
📬

I deliver

From day one
  • ✓Signed POD captured with the camera right at delivery.
  • ✓Quantity check: shortages, overages, damages or refusals are logged as an open case with their evidence (photos, notes) on the spot.
Phase 2
  • Full claims workflow: investigation, follow-up and formal resolution of every case.
6
💵

I get paid

From day one
  • ✓The invoice goes to the broker with everything attached: rate confirmation, POD and accessorials with their evidence (detention, lumper). Nothing to reconstruct later.
  • ✓The commission is calculated on its own with each broker's % and charged automatically (card). The broker pays you the freight directly, same as today.
Phase 2
  • Factoring / quick-pay: payment advanced for a fee, integrated into the platform.
7
🗄️

I close the load

From day one
  • ✓Closure checklist: the load doesn't get archived until BOL, POD, invoice and exceptions are complete. Nothing is left half-done.
  • ✓The closed load is archived and immutable — my complete backup for any dispute or audit, months later.
  • ✓At closure, the trip's numbers are calculated: on time or not, detention recovered, profitability.
Phase 2
  • Deep analysis of the accumulated history (see "Reports & analytics" below).

Platform-wide capabilities

What runs alongside every stage, all the time.

🎙️

Voice on the road (the flagship)

From day one
  • ✓Messages I send the driver in transit are announced and read out loud (CarPlay-style), and he replies by dictating. English and Spanish.
  • ✓It does not read documents or financial information — it's a road-safety feature, exactly as you defined it.
Phase 2
  • Voice expansions (e.g., a full conversational assistant).
💬

Communication

From day one
  • ✓Per-load messages between dispatcher and driver (the base the voice feature runs on), push and email notifications, and each load's event history.
Phase 2
  • Full hub: broker emails synced into each load's thread, SMS, AI phone calls to brokers, archive and audit of all communication.
🛡️

Compliance

From day one
  • ✓Carrier, driver and equipment documents with expirations watched (insurance, CDL, medical card, inspections, permits) and configurable early alerts.
  • ✓Automatic blocking: nobody gets dispatched with an expired document. Carrier verification against FMCSA.
Phase 2
  • Automatic reading of more documents (BOL, POD, third-party invoices), versioning, automated retention and audit packages.
📊

Reports & analytics

From day one
  • ✓A dashboard with the essentials of the operation: available trucks, loads in transit, pending confirmations, commissions, per-carrier profitability and history.
Phase 2
  • Executive dashboards, carrier/broker/driver scorecards, forecasting (volume, cash flow, margins) and AI recommendations.
⚙️

Administration

From day one
  • ✓Users with 3 roles: Admin, Dispatcher and Driver — everyone sees only what's theirs.
  • ✓I configure without a programmer: commission % per broker, expiration-alert windows, detention free time and my catalogs.
Phase 2
  • Advanced roles (accounting, compliance officer…), corporate security (MFA/SSO) and configurable approval workflows.
🌐

From your operation to a product

From day one
  • ✓The platform is born multi-company ready: your operation is the first one, with no rework later.
Phase 2
  • Sell access to other carriers and brokers — subscriptions, data isolated per company — with Truckers Corner as the first success story.

⚠️ Four clarifications — so there are no surprises

Voice does NOT read rate confirmations or full documents — only operational messages. It's a safety-at-the-wheel feature, with the scope you defined yourself.
The broker does not log into the system in v1. Everything that reaches him (your availability, the invoice with its evidence) travels by email — the way the industry works today. His portal is Phase 2.
The reefer temperature is reported by the driver from his app. Automatic sensors and telematics are Phase 2.
No factoring in v1. Payments keep working like today: the broker pays you directly. Factoring / quick-pay comes in Phase 2.
In one sentence: v1 ships complete and operational — it replaces your manual operation from day one. Phase 2 is not "what was left out": it's the growth into a product for the industry, it's built on top of v1 (nothing gets rewritten), and you choose which block and in what order. The v1 details live in the Quotation; the full Phase 2 picture, in the Phase 2 Roadmap.
