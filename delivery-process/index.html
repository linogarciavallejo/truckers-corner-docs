Truckers Corner platform · the working rhythm from first phase to launch

You never wait until the end to see anything, and you never review blind.

4

The preview link: a private, always-on URL where every phase appears as it's built — hosted at my own cost until your production environment starts in Phase 4. Development never stops while a review is open: each delivery is tagged and change-logged, so it's always clear what you're validating.

4 · Why what you validated stays validated

Every new version of the software goes through a pre-trip inspection — automatic, before it ever reaches you. Same idea as your drivers': you don't just check the new cargo, you walk around the whole truck.

Unit checks

Every money calculation, every business rule — hundreds of them, one by one.

Integration checks

The system against a real database — the way it runs in real life, not in theory.

End-to-end checks

A robot clicks through the portal like a person: post, dispatch, invoice.

Regression — your documents

Your own real rate confirmations are re-read on every single version, forever.

If anything you already validated breaks, the inspection fails and that version never leaves the yard. I find out — you don't have to.

How you'll see it: every piece of work lives on a shared status board (Linear) you can open any time. The moment an item shows ✓ Done it means exactly two things: it passed the full pre-trip inspection, and it's already published — on your preview link until production goes live in Phase 4, and on your production environment from then on. “Done” never means “done on my machine.”

What this means for the paperwork: because you see every phase twice before formally reviewing it — live on the preview, then in the demo — a 10-business-day review window per milestone is comfortable, and anything you're already using for real loads simply counts as accepted. The contract just writes down the way we actually work.
